Commercial Hydropower Mechanical Systems

Designed mechanical components and systems for multiple commercial hydropower projects.

Project Overview

Developed a custom experimental turbulence facility using independently controlled loudspeaker arrays and an active-grid wind tunnel. Investigated turbulence and coherent structure generation in laboratory-generated turbulent flows.

Engineering Contributions

  • Designed and developed experimental facilities.
  • Developed experimental control systems.
  • Performed PIV measurements.
  • Performed detailed turbulence analysis including POD.
  • Published peer-reviewed research.
