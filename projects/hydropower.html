Commercial Hydropower Mechanical Systems

Mechanical Design Engineer | Units Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. | 2018–2022

Designed mechanical components and systems for multiple commercial hydropower projects.

Project Overview

Designed mechanical and hydraulic structures for commercial hydropower projects, covering the design and engineering of water-control gates, penstock systems, trashracks, and mechanical hoisting equipment. My work involved taking components from initial engineering calculations and design development through detailed drawings and technical specifications. The systems I worked on included bulkhead gates, radial (Tainter) gates, fixed-wheel intake gates, penstocks, elbows, bifurcations, trashracks, and gate hoisting systems. Most of these projects have since been commissioned. Explore the commissioned hydropower projects: [Units Engineering Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. ]

Engineering Contributions

  • Hydraulic Gate Design: Performed structural and mechanical design of various gate systems, including radial (Tainter) gates, fixed-wheel intake gates, bulkhead gates, and trashracks.
  • Penstock and Piping Systems: Designed high-pressure steel penstocks, elbows, and complex bifurcations (wye branches). Applied finite element analysis (FEA) to evaluate structural integrity and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to investigate flow characteristics.
  • Mechanical Hoisting Systems: Designed wire-rope drum hoists and screw-jack hoists (stem hoists) for vertical and radial gates. Also developed procurement specifications and equipment interface details for vendor-supplied hydraulic cylinder hoists.
  • Engineering Standards and Design: Performed structural and mechanical design calculations using applicable Indian Standards (IS Codes), applying limit-state and allowable-stress design approaches.
